Overview

Traction power is the transit system's electrical backbone, and its substations generate a constant stream of SCADA telemetry. This research turns that raw telemetry into clean, standardized datasets — so power-factor, energy, and phase-level measurements compare consistently across substations for studying operating patterns and reliability.

What I'm building

  • Building Python pipelines in Jupyter to clean traction-power SCADA telemetry and standardize power-factor and energy metrics.
  • Writing SQL queries to extract phase-level measurements across the 3 phases, comparing substation operating patterns.
  • Automating preprocessing and data-quality checks to produce clean datasets for traction-power research.
