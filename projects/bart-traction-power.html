BART Traction-Power Pipelines

Python + SQL pipelines that clean SCADA telemetry and standardize energy metrics across electrical phases, producing analysis-ready datasets for traction-power reliability research at Bay Area Rapid Transit.

🚆 Bay Area Rapid Transit (BART) · Student Researcher · Aug 2026 – Present · Berkeley, CA
All projects

Overview

Traction power is the electrical backbone of the transit system, and the substations that supply it generate a continuous stream of SCADA telemetry. This research turns that raw, messy telemetry into clean, standardized datasets — so power-factor, energy, and phase-level measurements can be compared consistently across substations and used to study operating patterns and reliability.

What I'm building

  • Developing Python pipelines in Jupyter to clean and structure traction-power SCADA telemetry, standardizing power-factor and energy metrics for reliability analysis.
  • Writing SQL queries to extract aggregate and phase-level measurements across the three electrical phases, enabling comparison of substation operating patterns.
  • Automating preprocessing and data-quality checks with Python and AI-assisted workflows to produce clean datasets for traction-power research.
